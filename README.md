## Electrochemical research products

### Electrochemical cells

The [electrochemical cell collection](https://www.potentiolab.com/product-category/electrochemical-cells/) includes H-type, flow, sealed and configurable cell systems. A suitable cell design helps define the electrolyte volume, electrode spacing, gas environment, membrane separation and sampling arrangement used during an experiment.

Typical considerations include:

- Two-electrode or three-electrode configuration
- Divided or undivided chamber design
- Required electrolyte volume
- Working, reference and counter electrode placement
- Gas inlet, gas outlet and sampling ports
- Optical access or temperature-control requirements
- Materials compatible with the electrolyte and operating conditions

### Working electrodes

[Working electrodes](https://www.potentiolab.com/product-category/working-electrodes/) are available in materials and geometries used across electroanalytical and materials research. Options shown by PotentioLab include glassy carbon, platinum, gold, graphite and configurable electrode designs.

Working-electrode selection depends on the potential window, electrolyte chemistry, surface preparation method, current density and whether the experiment requires a disk, plate, rod or custom geometry.

### Reference electrodes

The [reference electrode range](https://www.potentiolab.com/product-category/reference-electrodes/) includes Ag/AgCl, saturated calomel, Hg/HgO, reversible hydrogen and other reference systems. Selecting a reference electrode requires attention to the electrolyte, junction design, temperature, expected potential range and possible contamination of the test solution.

Researchers should also record the exact reference system and filling solution when reporting measured potentials so that results remain comparable.

### Counter electrodes

[Counter electrodes](https://www.potentiolab.com/product-category/counter-electrode/) include platinum, graphite and other geometries for completing an electrochemical circuit. Surface area, material stability and placement should be considered so the counter reaction does not limit the working-electrode process or interfere with the measurement.

### Screen-printed and interdigitated electrodes

PotentioLab also provides [screen-printed electrodes](https://www.potentiolab.com/product-category/screen-printed-electrodes/) and [interdigitated electrodes and IDE sensors](https://www.potentiolab.com/product-category/interdigitated-electrode/). These compact platforms can support sensor development, impedance measurements, surface modification studies and experiments where repeatable electrode geometry is important.

## How to specify an electrochemical setup

Before requesting a quotation, it is helpful to prepare a short experiment summary. Include the intended electrochemical method, electrolyte, electrode materials, sample dimensions, temperature range, atmosphere, expected current and any dimensional restrictions imposed by existing equipment.

A practical specification checklist is:

1. Describe the experiment and the measurement objective.
2. State whether a two-electrode or three-electrode arrangement is required.
3. List all materials that will contact the electrolyte.
4. Provide electrode dimensions and preferred geometry.
5. Define the electrolyte volume and number of chambers.
6. Note gas, vacuum, heating, cooling or optical-access needs.
7. Identify the potentiostat, battery tester or other instrument connection.
8. Include a drawing or photo when the hardware must interface with an existing setup.

This information helps the technical team evaluate whether an available product is suitable and reduces unnecessary changes during quotation.

## Configurable and custom laboratory hardware

Standard catalog hardware is useful when a well-established cell format meets the experimental requirement. Customization may be appropriate when a researcher needs a non-standard sample size, special port arrangement, alternative construction material, controlled electrode spacing or integration with another instrument.

For a custom request, provide dimensions, material preferences, operating conditions and a description of how the assembly will be used. PotentioLab can then review the request and discuss a suitable configuration.
